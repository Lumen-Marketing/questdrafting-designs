How it goes, in order

Six steps on one agreement. The line between them is the part most projects drop.

  1. 01

    Survey and setbacks

    What the lot and the zoning will allow, established before anything is drawn.

  2. 02

    Design

    From first massing study to a resolved floor plan, shaped by your lot, your zoning, and how you actually live.

  3. 03

    3D model

    A full 3D model and photoreal renderings, day and twilight, before a single construction sheet is final.

  4. 04

    Construction documents

    Around 22 sheets across roughly six disciplines, drawn to the 2024 IBC / IRC / IECC code cycle.

  5. 05

    Permit submitted

    Submittals formatted to each jurisdiction's checklist.

  6. 06

    Permit in hand

    Plan review comments answered, resubmittals managed until the permit is in hand.
